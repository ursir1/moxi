# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** November 2026

## 1. Overview

Moxi is an open-source application. We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains what data is collected and how it is used.

## 2. Data We Do Not Collect

Moxi contributors **do not** collect, store, or sell any personal data. The application is open source — you can verify this by reviewing the [source code](https://github.com/ursir1/moxi).

## 3. Stremio Account Data

If you sign in with a Stremio account, your credentials and library are handled by **Stremio (Smart Code OOD)**. Please review [Stremio's Privacy Policy](https://www.stremio.com/privacy) for details on how they handle your data.

## 4. Local Storage

Moxi stores your preferences and settings locally on your device. This data never leaves your machine unless synced via your Stremio account.

## 5. Third-Party Addons

Addons are third-party services. Each addon may have its own privacy practices. Moxi has no control over what data addons collect when you use them.

## 6. Analytics & Tracking

Moxi does not include any analytics, tracking pixels, or advertising SDKs.

## 7. Children's Privacy

Moxi is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always available at [github.com/ursir1/moxi](https://github.com/ursir1/moxi).

## 9. Contact

For privacy concerns, open an issue at [github.com/ursir1/moxi/issues](https://github.com/ursir1/moxi/issues).
